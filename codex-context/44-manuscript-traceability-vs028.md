# VS028 Manuscript Traceability
**Status:** Frozen

The manuscript supports operational alerts for maintenance/PMS attention and low vehicle availability. VS028 implements these as canonical in-app notifications.

Maintenance authority is canonical readiness. Low availability uses canonical current availability. Any unconfirmed numeric threshold is configurable/provisional, not client-confirmed.

External email delivery is outside VS028. After implementation, MIC-019 may be marked IMPLEMENTED if both notification families are operational and deduplicated.
