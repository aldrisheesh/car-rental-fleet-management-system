## CQ-031 — Pickup / Return Reminder Lead Time
**Status:** Open — researcher-designed baseline awaiting client validation

Current baseline:
- pickup reminder: 24h before;
- return reminder: 24h before;
- overdue: first detection after due, then at most once per Asia/Manila calendar day while physically unreturned.

Question:
> For pickup and return reminders, we currently plan to notify one day before the scheduled time. Is that enough for your operation, or would you prefer a different lead time?

Client feedback may later change timing/cadence without redesigning the notification architecture.

Existing important open items remain CQ-028, CQ-029, CQ-030, CQ-017, and CQ-026.
