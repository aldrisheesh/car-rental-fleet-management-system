## Access model and shared surfaces

- Canonical roles are `Owner/Admin`, `Operations Staff`, and `Customer/Renter`. Server APIs resolve an active Supabase principal from HTTP-only access/refresh cookies; a readable view cookie drives legacy client guards.
- `/admin` renders `AdminShell`. It checks the client-visible principal after render and redirects unauthenticated users to `/sign-in`. Staff navigation is limited to Dashboard, Bookings, Calendar, Notifications, Reports, and Profile. Owner/Admin additionally sees Decision Support, Customers, Payments, Fleet, Maintenance, Audit Trail, Users & Roles, and Branches. `/admin/settings` exists but is not linked.
- The public `Header` links Home, Vehicles, Booking, Contact, and, for a signed-in customer, Customer Dashboard and Profile. Its sign-in/sign-up dialog is also the complete `/sign-in` surface. Admin/Staff sign-in routes to `/admin`; customer sign-in routes to `/customer-landing` unless a booking handoff overrides it.
- `NotificationsPanel` is embedded in `/customer` and is the body of `/admin/notifications`. It loads recipient-scoped notifications, marks one read, and lets customers toggle transactional email. Notification links route only to coarse modules (`/customer`, `/payment-details`, `/admin/bookings`, `/admin/payments`, `/admin/maintenance`, or `/admin`).
- Root-level 404 and error surfaces offer Go home and, for errors, Try again. They do not expose role-aware recovery.

## Duplicate capability and legacy-presentation findings

1. `/` and `/customer-landing` are duplicate marketing/search pages. Header navigation also exposes Vehicles and Booking as competing starts.
2. There is no vehicle-detail route; a card and the editable Booking select jointly stand in for selection/detail.
3. Requirements review is duplicated in `/admin/bookings` and `/admin/customers`; maintenance creation is duplicated in Fleet and Maintenance.
4. Customer payment, requirements, notifications, bookings, and rental status are independent top-level/stacked modules with no per-booking source-of-truth route.
5. Admin lifecycle work is selected-row state on `/admin/bookings`, not deep-linkable detail. Payment review remains isolated.
6. Customers, Home, customer landing, fallback fleet data, profile storage, Contact, and Settings contain static/simulated assumptions alongside canonical server-backed modules.
7. Several visible controls have no implementation: booking Export/More filters, Customers Add customer, global Admin search/command hint, Settings controls, and Contact delivery.
