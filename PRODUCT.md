# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Customers request rentals and submit payment proof. Owner/Admin users review payments, confirm eligible bookings, and resolve operational exceptions.

## Product Purpose

Briah's Car Rental manages the customer rental journey and the operations work required to fulfill it.

## Operating Context

Owner/Admin users review a payment proof alongside the booking, customer, payment method, and saved booking quote. A verified payment can automatically confirm an eligible booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Payment review uses the booking-specific amount due, transaction reference, uploaded proof, and a saved quote snapshot. Direct payment links must remain reviewable without losing access to the full payment queue.

## Brand Commitments

Briah's Car Rental uses a restrained forest-green and ivory visual language with clear operational hierarchy.

## Evidence on Hand

The project contains customer and Owner/Admin payment workflows, booking records, payment proofs, payment methods, and booking quote data.

## Product Principles

- Keep customer payment submission simple and exact.
- Give operations staff the evidence needed to make a confident review decision.
- Preserve a clear path from payment review to booking confirmation.
