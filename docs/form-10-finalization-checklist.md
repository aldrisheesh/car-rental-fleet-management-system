## Study details to preserve

- Main evaluation: **70 customers, 2 administrators, 1 Operations Staff member, and 3 IT experts — 76 participants in total.** Pilot participants are separate and excluded from the main evaluation.
- Operational records used for development, demonstrations, and technical testing are **synthetic**. Actual participant consent records, questionnaire responses, and comments are human-participant research data.
- Forecasting and fleet allocation support decisions; authorized business users retain operational approval.
- Staff evaluate only tasks and features they can access or appropriately observe.
- The revised questionnaire has **38 customer statements, 43 business-user statements, and 37 IT-expert statements**, totaling 118 statements across three forms.
- Questionnaire responses use the existing four-point agreement scale. Unassessable items remain unanswered and are excluded from the applicable denominator; they are not scored as zero or treated as an additional response category.
- Validation, pilot testing, recruitment, and evaluation remain described as planned unless there are genuine records showing their completion.
