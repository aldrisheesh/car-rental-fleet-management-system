# Owner-approved payment hold policy

Approved in the implementation conversation on October 14, 2026.

- New final quotes hold the exact vehicle and rental interval for 24 elapsed hours after requirements verification and a fresh availability check. A request alone does not reserve a vehicle.
- For pickup within 24 hours, the owner must set an explicit future payment deadline before handover. Standard quotes cannot silently change the 24-hour window; a later extension requires a recorded reason.
- Payment proof must finish submission before the recorded deadline. Timely proof protects the vehicle during review, even after the original deadline. Review is due within ten operating hours (one business day), using the established daily 8 AM–6 PM Asia/Manila schedule. Overdue review alerts the active owner; it never automatically releases the vehicle.
- The first correction request allows six operating hours. A further failed correction requires an owner extension and a fresh availability check; it does not restart an indefinite automatic hold.
- Expiry releases only the temporary inventory hold. The booking request, quote, submitted proof history and payment records remain. No cancellation, refund, forfeiture, penalty or new fee is generated by hold expiry.
- Owner extensions require a reason and a future deadline before pickup. Every extension rechecks availability, including an expired hold. Original quote amounts and payment evidence remain unchanged.
- Initial quotation, halfway and two-hour reminders are delivered through the existing in-app notification and email outbox. Reminders stop once proof is under review. Generation-based event keys deduplicate repeated scheduler invocations. A late scheduler sends the most relevant remaining warning rather than both warnings at once.
- Pre-policy quotes remain grandfathered. They receive no retroactive deadline. Reissuing an unpaid quote after activation creates a new hold and sends the ordinary quotation notification.

## Integrity and rollout

Hold conflicts are checked under the same vehicle advisory locks used for assignment, confirmation and date changes. Public catalog/Finder, rescheduling, DSS supply and transfer candidate checks include active holds. Branch reassignment is blocked while a vehicle has an active hold. Browser roles cannot write holds, history or invoke hold mutation RPCs.

Hold event snapshots retain owner reasons, actor and timestamps. Review/verified holds have no automatic expiry; an owner handles exceptional readiness or confirmation problems through the existing resolution workflow.

The policy migration installs with activation disabled. The scheduler migration creates an inactive ten-minute Supabase Cron job because the current Vercel Hobby deployment only supports daily cron. The scheduled endpoint uses a dedicated `PAYMENT_HOLD_PROCESSOR_SECRET`, stored in Vercel and Supabase Vault (`briah_payment_hold_processor`); existing cron credentials remain untouched. Enable both only after production deployment and a protected-endpoint check.
